## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-06

### Added

- Local-first multi-account finance workspace.
- Transactions, transfers, categories, tags and search.
- Budgets, savings goals, debts and recurring rules.
- AES-256-GCM encrypted local vault and encrypted backups.
- JSON and CSV portability with validation and formula-injection protection.
- On-device PDF reports, privacy mode and PWA offline shell.
- Responsive desktop and mobile interface.
- Unit, browser, accessibility-ready and security CI foundations.
